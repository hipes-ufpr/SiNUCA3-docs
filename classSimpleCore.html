Detailed Description

SimpleCore fetches an instruction from the required parameter fetching, a Component<InstructionPacket> and optionally queries two memories (without caring with the result at all) with the instruction. The two memories are passed as the Component<MemoryPacket> parameters instructionMemory and dataMemory.
